Before you start

Questions people actually ask.

Do I need any design experience?

No. Type your brand name, pick a register that fits — streetwear, elegant, vintage, athletic, and more — and ZVAKHO generates real artwork from real fonts and icons. If you can already design, you can also upload your own artwork instead.

What does it actually cost to start?

Generating your brand identity is free. You only spend money when you're actually printing and selling — there's no monthly fee just to have a store sitting there, and no inventory to buy upfront.

Do I need to buy stock or hold inventory?

No. Shirts are printed after a customer orders, not before. You're never sitting on boxes of stock you're hoping will sell.

How do I actually get paid?

Connect your own Paynow or EcoCash account. When a customer pays, it goes straight there — ZVAKHO never holds your money or sits in the middle of the transaction.

Can I use my own logo instead of a generated one?

Yes. Upload your own artwork any time, and reposition, resize, or rescale it inside the real print area before it goes to production.

What if I want to sell in bulk, not one shirt at a time?

That's what wholesale is for — see below. Bulk production runs bring your per-unit cost down once you're past one-at-a-time orders.

Selling in real volume? Let's talk wholesale.

Once you're past one-shirt-at-a-time, bulk production runs bring your per-unit cost down. Get in touch and we'll walk you through it.
